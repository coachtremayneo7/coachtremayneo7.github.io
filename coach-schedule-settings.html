Scheduling settings

Weekly hours, regular-client booking access, and the weekly limit for regular clients. All times are Central Time.

Coach key

Weekly hours

Each day can have up to two blocks here. Blocks start on the quarter hour and must be at least 45 minutes long. Empty days are closed.

Changing hours does not cancel or change sessions that already exist. New sessions must fit the hours you save.

Regular-client weekly limit

How many sessions a regular client may book in one Monday-to-Sunday week. Challenge clients keep their limit of 3.

Client booking access

A client can book only after you turn their access on. Turning access off stops new bookings; existing bookings stay in place and can still be cancelled by the client. Clients must also be active.
