How it runs

3 sessions a week. ~20 minutes each. Do them at a truck stop, rest area, or hotel — no gym needed.

Gear: just you. A sturdy step (cab step, curb), your tailgate, and a couple water jugs cover everything. A cheap resistance band helps but isn't required.

Every day: the "Undo the Drive" reset below. This is the one that saves your back.

⚡ Daily — Undo the Drive (5 min)
  • Standing hip-flexor stretch — 30 sec each side
  • Standing t-spine rotation — 10 each side
  • Glute bridges — 15 reps
  • 10-minute walk after your shift
Before you start: this is general fitness info, not medical advice. Train within your ability, warm up, and stop if anything hurts — sharp pain is a stop sign, not something to push through. If you've got a health condition, an injury, or it's been a while, clear it with your doctor first (smart move before your next DOT physical anyway).
