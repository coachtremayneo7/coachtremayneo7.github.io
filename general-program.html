How it runs

3 sessions a week, ~30 minutes each — full-body strength plus a short conditioning finisher. The strength work is what keeps your muscle while the fat comes off.

Gear: a pair of dumbbells (or a resistance band) covers it. No dumbbells? Every move has a bodyweight version — just do the reps slow.

Every day: the daily habit below. Fat loss is won outside the workout as much as in it.

⚡ Daily — the base
  • Get your steps — aim for a daily walk, and a 10-minute one after your biggest meal
  • Protein with every meal — it keeps you full and protects muscle
  • Fill half your plate with veggies before anything else
  • 7–8 hours sleep — short sleep sabotages fat loss
Before you start: this is general fitness info, not medical or nutrition advice, and it's not a substitute for personalized care. Train within your ability, warm up, and stop if anything hurts — sharp pain is a stop sign. Aim for steady, sustainable change, not extreme restriction. If you have a health condition, an injury, are pregnant, or it's been a while, check with your doctor first.

How it runs

4 sessions a week — Upper, Lower, Upper, Lower. ~40 minutes each. This split hits every muscle twice a week, which is where growth comes from.

Gear: dumbbells and a bench (or gym access) is ideal. Home with just bands? Every move has a swap — the key is pushing close to failure.

Every day: the daily habit below. You don't grow in the gym — you grow eating and sleeping after it.

⚡ Daily — the base
  • Protein with every meal — it's the raw material for muscle
  • Eat enough — building needs fuel, don't train hungry
  • 7–8 hours sleep — this is when the muscle actually gets built
  • Add a little weight or a rep whenever a set feels easy — that's the whole game
Before you start: this is general fitness info, not medical or nutrition advice, and it's not a substitute for personalized care. Warm up, keep good form, and stop if anything hurts — sharp pain is a stop sign. If you have a health condition, an injury, or it's been a while, check with your doctor first.
