Coach TreMayne Alerts — SMS Terms & Conditions

Provided by Quantum Flex Training, LLC, Bowling Green, Kentucky

Effective date: September 29, 2026

  1. Program description. Coach TreMayne Alerts sends informational text messages to enrolled Coach TreMayne coaching clients about class status (for example, whether a class is on or canceled), schedule changes, and training reminders. We do not send marketing or promotional texts through this program.
  2. How to join. Enrolled clients join by entering their mobile number and checking the SMS consent box on our opt-in page. Consent is not a condition of purchase.
  3. Message frequency. Message frequency varies depending on your coaching schedule and program activity.
  4. Cost. Message and data rates may apply.
  5. How to opt out. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Twilio will send a confirmation that you have been unsubscribed. You may also reply UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE, or OPTOUT, or opt out by emailing coach@coachtremayne.com or telling your coach.
  6. How to rejoin. Reply START to resubscribe.
  7. Help. Reply HELP for help, or email coach@coachtremayne.com.
  8. Carriers. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  9. Eligibility. You must be the account holder for the mobile number, or have the account holder's permission.
  10. Privacy. Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy at https://coachtremayne.com/privacy.html.
  11. Changes. We may update these terms. Changes will be posted on this page.
