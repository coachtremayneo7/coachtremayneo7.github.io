⚠ Loading rail

Cleared means you can load — flares mean load light and watch. Anything that reproduces pain gets pulled that day.

The next-day check-in is your gauge — pain-free tomorrow = earn the next notch. Flare = back off. That's how you find the line.

Long-haul driver = hours seated daily. That's the root — locked hips, dead glutes, compressed spine. Every session fights the truck.
