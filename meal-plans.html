Meal plans

Coach tool

Enter your coach key

The same key you use for Coach Hub. It stays in this browser tab only and is cleared when you close the tab or press Lock.

Choose a client

Pick a client from the list at the top to open their meal plan and grocery list.

Import a plan (JSON)

Paste or choose a plan or template file. It is checked and previewed first, then loaded into this editor. Nothing is saved until you press Save plan.

    Save as reusable template

    Downloads what's in this editor as a JSON file you can import for other clients. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    Copy from another client

    Loads their meal plan into this editor as a starting point. Nothing is saved until you press Save plan. Their grocery list is never copied.

    Grocery note

    Shown at the top of the client's grocery list. Saved separately from the plan.

    Grocery list

    Each change saves right away. The client sees the same list.
